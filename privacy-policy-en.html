Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 25, 2026

Pronoka is a free app that lets friends predict tennis match outcomes (ATP, US Open and other tournaments) and compare scores in private leagues. No real money is involved — it's a points-based prediction game, not gambling, and it contains no in-app purchases tied to real-money wagering.

This page explains what data is collected, why, and how you can control it.

1. Who processes your data

Pronoka is developed and operated by an independent developer. For any question about your data, see the "Contact" section at the bottom of this page.

2. Data we collect

Pronoka never asks for payment information and does not collect precise location data, phone contacts, or health data.

3. How your data is used

  • Operating your account and your predictions;
  • Calculating your points and rankings (global and among friends);
  • Displaying your profile (stats, history) to members of leagues you share with them;
  • Letting you create or join private tournaments via an invite code.

Pronoka never shows another user's exact prediction on a match before it's finished, and never reveals prediction details to an unrelated third-party user.

4. Sharing data with third parties

Your data is never sold or shared for advertising purposes. It is only sent to the technical providers strictly necessary to run the app:

  • Supabase (database hosting, authentication) — stores your account, predictions, and points;
  • Apple (Sign in with Apple) — if you choose to sign in with your Apple ID;
  • Google (Google Sign-In) — if you choose to sign in with your Google account.

Odds and match information shown in the app come from third-party tennis data providers (public sports APIs); these providers never receive any personal data about you.

Pronoka does not include any advertising SDK or marketing tracking tool.

5. Data retention

Your data is kept for as long as your account exists. You can delete your account at any time directly from the app (Profile → Settings → Delete my account), which removes your profile, predictions, and associated points.

6. Your rights

Under GDPR (for users located in the European Union) and equivalent laws elsewhere, you have the right to access, correct, delete, and export your data. To exercise these rights, contact us (see below) or use the built-in account deletion in the app.

7. Children

Pronoka is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from children of that age.

8. Security

Data is stored with Supabase under strict access control (each user can only read data they're authorized to access). Communication between the app and the server is encrypted (HTTPS).

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated occasionally, for example when a new feature is added. The last-updated date is shown at the top of this page.
